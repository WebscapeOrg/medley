Medley

A sound for every herdr workspace and status.

Settings

Medley handles notification sounds while enabled. Disabling or using Uninstall Medley here restores herdr’s previous sound setting. Audio plays on the herdr server’s machine, including for the workspace you are viewing.

Local settings · No account or cloud service required

My Sounds

Choose an MP3 or WAV to import it immediately, then select it in any workspace. WAV files convert to MP3. Maximum 20 MB.

My workspaces

Choose a workspace and a status. Changes save immediately. Default follows herdr’s configured completion or attention sound; Working and Unknown are quiet by default.
